# Lagos Green Maker

Emmanuel’s Lagos plant and tower research catalogue.

Live catalogue: https://emmanuelajibade751-beep.github.io/lagos-green-maker/

## Reading the catalogue

Each of the 100 plants has three original growth-stage illustrations, container information, and sourced care and timing records. Open a plant to use the Plant, Balcony, Tower, Care, and Evidence tabs. Timing ranges retain their evidence qualifications; the illustrations are not calibrated biological growth predictions.

The Tower tab adds native whole-tower and detail renders, measured placement-fit notes, and stage-specific solar results. Render source and critic receipts are linked beside the images. `catalogue_validation.json` records the packaged render coverage and checks.

## Solar scope

The solar collection contains 300 whole-tower cases and 300 independent local-placement cases: 100 species at three stages. Whole-tower cases repeat an approved native arrangement on 384 balconies, or on 800 vertical facade slots for the 11 wall species. Local cases place one module independently at 12 positions; they are not a simultaneously planted tower.

The unplanted architectural baseline remains separate. Annual irradiation, direct-beam diagnostics, sunlight hours, and shade snapshots have distinct meanings. The model treats evaluated plant geometry as opaque. These results do not establish temperature reduction, thermal comfort, plant health, or structural suitability. Missing diagnostic input provenance is labelled where applicable.

Two developed-stage irradiation comparisons (Silver inch and pennywort) include 3D axonometric sheets with a common 0–1,800 kWh/m²/year scale. These sheets are shown only for their matching species, stage, and whole-tower scope.

## Geometry and fit

Native plant and holder dimensions and materials are preserved. The approved Silver inch arrangement has two rows and 26 pots. Measured conflicts with the tower bays remain visible and labelled; fit checks are not structural, load, drainage, or access approvals. Tall crowns use a labelled wider camera frame without scaling the plants.

This repository contains browser assets and review evidence. Editable Blender and Rhino production files remain in the project workspace. Serve this directory over HTTP for local review; GitHub Pages publishes the root of `main`.
