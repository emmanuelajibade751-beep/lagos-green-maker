# Numbered tower and fixed plant views

The interactive viewer is the **unplanted architectural baseline**. It preserves the exact 1,600 source voxel bounds and IDs, 384 checkerboard balcony transforms, 15 balcony parts, 64 surrounding buildings and 93 site solids. A white tower finish and fine voxel-grid annotation implement the requested presentation; the source Rhino model is untouched. All other saved Rhino display colours remain recorded and used.

`host_validation.json` records geometry verification. `host.js` includes source GUIDs, colours and provenance. `solar_data.js` retains all 1,600 voxel records, including 864 cells without a sampled exterior face. Baseline results do not change when species changes. Null solar metrics are unavailable measurements, not zero exposure. Rankings refer to annual incident irradiation, not temperature, and preserve all tied extrema.

The planted views are separate native Blender renders. Original geometry, modifiers, procedural materials and textures are linked unchanged and positioned using the audited assembly transforms. The approved two-row Silver Inch study is the sole active LAG-983 layout. Eleven approved wall-mounted assemblies use 800 vertical facade bays, with no roof planting; other assemblies use the 384 balcony placements. The tested architectural host remains unchanged. Planting overlap and recessed wall media are design coordination issues, not resolved engineering details.

The architectural source is `14D_Tower_Checkerboard_Balconies.3dm`, SHA-256 `adc2ede89e2690905b5a33f6ac85127ce2d92ca111bbe397d78da85c957c104d`. The scenario uses a schematic neighbourhood and assumed north, not a surveyed site. The original three plant stages and botanical evidence remain in the catalogue.

Visual reference: The Why Factory, [The Green Dip](https://thewhyfactory.com/project/the-green-dip/), with the supplied Perch reference credited to the 2019 TU Delft print, page 165. Fine square grid linework is a presentation annotation over unchanged 4 m cubes.

Experimental simplified plant exports were rejected and are not part of the published catalogue. Fixed renders preserve the authored assets; they are not species-specific solar simulations.
