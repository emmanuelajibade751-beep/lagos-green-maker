## Review rubric

- **Source and coverage:** Exactly01/02/03 per species; image paths and current receipts recorded. Counts alone never confer visual acceptance.
- **Framing:** Entire intended plant and vessel fit with usable margins; no clipped canopy, roots or vessel. Backdrop edges must not obscure the study.
- **Visibility:** Plant silhouette, primary organs and vessel visibly present; lighting/background adequate. Full frame inspected where sheet cannot resolve a concern.
- **Comparison:** Shared camera scale, direction and framing within each triplet; changing plant size is legible. Scale need not match across species.
- **Botanical stage:** Flag conspicuous identity, attachment, architecture, foliage-density, size/proportion or stage-removal anomalies. Declining height is not automatically an error; measured ordinal stage is not chronological age.
- **Container:** Check visible vessel/support/root-zone presence and obvious penetrations or floating attachments; no engineering-performance inference.
- **Decision:** QUALIFIED_PASS, RERENDER_REQUIRED, MODEL_REVIEW_REQUIRED or PENDING. A qualification cannot excuse clipping, missing parts or conspicuously wrong form.
