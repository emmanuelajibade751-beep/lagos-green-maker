Shrub studies review

This rebuild responds to the shrub critique. Earlier files remain in shrubs_12. It uses the Plant Modeller's existing geometry at its saved scale; it does not recalibrate growth or establish plant ages.

What changed

  • Acalypha, Croton, Ixora, Hibiscus rosa-sinensis and Clerodendrum: six establishment plants in two trough rows, then six separate round pots in those same two rows, then larger floor pots. Intermediate pots are smaller than final pots.
  • Ocimum and Senna: one low row of three young plants, three individual intermediate pots, then two larger floor pots. Senna no longer has an automatic ground destination.
  • Murraya: the same low-row sequence, ending in larger pots. The developed plant plus its container rises just above the 4 m bay, so this stage needs open space above. No plant was shrunk to fit.
  • Gardenia: individual pots at all three stages, increasing in size. NYBG supports repotting; NC State identifies sensitivity to root disturbance. Keeping each rootball separate is our design response.
  • Roselle: three plants rather than two in one retained crop bed, with 1.10 m centre spacing. The bed stays through growth and harvest.
  • Pigeon pea: separate young containers, then an early transfer into a larger container retained for the remaining stages. ICRISAT supports young-seedling transplantation; full-size growth in the modelled final container remains unverified.
  • Cassava: separate young containers, then a receiving container retained through further growth. NC State supports container culture. Avoiding a later transfer of developing storage roots is our design choice; yield and pot size are not validated.

Review findings

  1. Six Ixora plants remain visible at the intermediate stage. The smaller pots retain the trough-row arrangement.
  2. Developed views show fewer plants per bay where necessary. The gallery states how many receiving bays are needed to retain the original plants.
  3. Upper rows are set behind lower rows, with clearance above lower foliage. This is a geometry check, not proof of daily sunlight on a real facade.
  4. Clerodendrum developed plants are spaced 2.80 m apart, responding to NParks' 2.5-3 m landscape guidance. Young rows are a temporary arrangement.
  5. The developed plant models are scenarios. They are not verified maximum species sizes or confirmed container-grown specimens.
  6. All holder capacities are calculated from model geometry, not derived from measured rootballs. The container schedule makes this distinction explicit.
  7. Water outlets, guards and support rails communicate the arrangement only. They are not construction details. Soil loads, wind, fixings and a working drainage route are not certified.
  8. No active Rhino or Grasshopper session was accessed.

Completion boundary

This is a revised arrangement/model set with sources and calculated container capacities. It is not a horticulturally validated, structurally approved planting specification. The remaining limitation is actual root-space and long-term performance data, not missing visual stages.
