# Age-to-size research — 100 plants

Research checked 2026-11-14. 124 source/cohort records and 264 source observations; 100 species-specific search dispositions; 300 current model stages assessed.

**21 stages across 13 species have conditional height-to-time analogs. 279 stages remain unassigned. Zero stages have a calibrated model birth age.**

The existing catalogue and earlier simulator remain separate. A conditional height analog is a research annotation, not proof that the model and its reproductive organs have the stated age. Open-ground, nursery, pruning and treatment clocks cannot be silently transferred into the catalogue containers.

## Matching method

Only newly verified candidate records with comparable whole-plant vertical height can match actual Model top Z, never design targets. Exact reported size intervals can supply their stated time interval. Adjacent means within the same cohort or explicitly identified institutional trajectory can supply only the full time bracket, assuming continuous monotone change within that interval. Rising and falling intervals remain distinct alternatives. There is no linear point-age inversion, extrapolation, rate inversion, treatment splicing, or forced age order across ordinal stages.

All outputs retain source units, origin of clock, starting stock, site/treatment, provenance and limitations. Unassigned ages are null in JSON and blank in CSV, never zero. Population means and standard errors are not individual prediction intervals. Ambiguous source clocks, incomplete graph extraction and inaccessible papers are explicit gaps. A search result without an extractable full-text pair does not become a verified numerical age.

The matched intervals do not establish canopy width, branching, roots, flowers, fruit, longevity, container suitability or lifespan. Field measurements in the intended Lagos growing conditions are still required for a predictive model. Record propagation date, planting date and initial size separately, then repeated total height, crown axes, stem diameter, phenological state, container volume, irrigation, substrate and pruning history.
