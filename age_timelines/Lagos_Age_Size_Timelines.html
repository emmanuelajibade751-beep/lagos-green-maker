These are conditional size-matched time ranges, not verified model ages. The models are synthetic. Source clocks may start at sowing, transplanting, cutting or monitoring. No stage has a calibrated birth age, and no source calibrates growth in the exact catalogue container. A height match does not establish flowering, fruiting, branching, root development or lifespan. Separate source alternatives remain separate.

Ranges between observations assume continuous monotone change within that interval. They are not fitted curves, confidence intervals or predictions for Lagos. No extrapolation or mature-height ÷ annual-rate calculation is used. Missing means not established by this search, not proof that evidence does not exist. Nursery-only records, inaccessible papers and graphs still requiring numerical extraction are shown explicitly.
